# Eureka Khong

Most portfolios are built to answer a simple question: **what do you do?**

This one is interested in a slightly different question: **who are you, what do you care about, and how do you move through the world?**

**Eureka Khong** is a sustainability practitioner working across people, places, ideas, and the environment. Her work brings together different interests, experiences, and ways of thinking. This website brings some of those pieces together in one place.

It is a portfolio, but not quite in the usual sense. Alongside professional work are the things Eureka is interested in, the places and ideas that have shaped her, and the things she notices along the way.

## More Than a Portfolio

A conventional portfolio tends to present finished work in neat categories.

This space leaves more room for what sits between the projects: the questions that shape the work, the interests that keep her curious, and the different ways she engages with the world.

Projects, writing, photography, observations, and other fragments can sit alongside one another without needing to fit neatly into a single category.

The aim is not to explain Eureka completely. It is simply to bring together enough of what she does and what interests her to offer a sense of how she thinks and works.

## What Lives Here

The website brings together:

* Sustainability work and projects
* Writing and essays
* Photography
* Journal entries
* Ideas and observations
* Personal interests
* Other work, experiments, and curiosities

Some things may become finished bodies of work. Others may remain fragments or passing curiosities.

The website is designed to make room for both.

## The Experience

The visual design is quiet, spacious, and considered. It gives the content room to breathe while allowing the site itself to have a distinct character.

An ash and light-blue base is paired with **Panash orange** as an accent. Typography, spacing, borders, and movement are used deliberately rather than decoratively.

The result is meant to feel personal, curious, and editorial rather than like a conventional professional portfolio.

## Technology

The website is a custom-built digital portfolio using:

* React
* TypeScript
* Vite
* TanStack Router
* Tailwind CSS
* Supabase
* Cloudinary
* Vercel
* GitHub

The technology stays mostly in the background. It provides the structure for a space that can evolve alongside Eureka's work and interests.
